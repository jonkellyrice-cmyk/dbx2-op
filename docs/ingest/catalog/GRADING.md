# How the tactical grades were set

Each Super, Ultimate, Evasive, Awoken, Super Soul and QQ Bang gets a 0–5 grade on each of the eight goals (spec §20.2: 1 = narrow or hard to use, 3 = broadly practical, 5 = highly reliable). The scorer turns a grade into 65% of the goal's score.

The grades in `catalog.json` are **MODEL** judgments: evidence `status: "model"`, `verification: "derived"`. Each one rests only on what the record already holds (the wiki's damage figure, hit count, Ki cost in bars, and whether it breaks guard, grabs, counters or teleports), and each says why in its `reason`. A goal that nothing recorded bears on stays **unknown**, never 0. Play testing can replace any grade later; change the grade and its reason together.

## From option scores to a loadout's score

For each goal the engine stacks the loadout's option scores: the best counts fully, the second at 35%, the third at 15% and each further one at 5%. A loadout has ten option slots, so that stack passes 100 easily. Xenoverse 2 therefore runs it through a curve that keeps rising and never reaches 100 (`XV2_COVERAGE_CURVE` in `src/domain/xv2/adapter.ts`): a stack of 60 scores 53, 100 scores 71, 135 scores 82 and 185 scores 90. The curve is a MODEL choice, not a game fact; tune it against real builds.

| Goal | What sets the grade |
|---|---|
| Burst damage | Measured damage in the community damage tests (`damage-tests.json`: level 99, 125 points, +5 QQ Bang): under 2,000 → 1, 2,000–3,999 → 2, 4,000–6,999 → 3, 7,000–9,999 → 4, 10,000 or more → 5. A cell that holds a range, a per-hit figure or a multiplier is graded on the first figure it lists, which is the damage at the skill's base cost; a measured 0 is grade 1. Only where a skill has no measured row, the wiki's damage per use: under 8% → 1, 8–14% → 2, 15–24% → 3, 25–34% → 4, 35% or more → 5. A source that only calls the damage low → 1. A Power Up's temporary buff to skill damage: about 8%, or an (S) boost → 2; only 3–4% → 1; a buff whose size the source doesn't state is not graded. A Power Up that stops you using skills while it lasts → 1. A QQ Bang's +5 to one kind of Super adds only about 6.66% damage with no attribute points in it, less with points (wiki), paid for by the other kind → 2. |
| Sustained damage | One to five hits → 1; a multi-hit beam or a barrage fired once (six or more hits) → 2; ten or more tracking hits, or a volley you can keep firing → 3; a long multi-hit Ultimate (15 or more hits) → 4; a Power Up's temporary buff of about a third to basic attacks → 3 (one of unstated size is not graded); a QQ Bang's +5 to both Basic Attacks and Ki Blast Supers (about 6.66% each at most) → 2. |
| Ki economy | Upfront cost: 1 bar → 4, 2 bars → 3, 3 bars → 2, 4 or more → 1. A cost you choose (1 to 5 bars) → 3. No Ki cost, but usable only once → 4; no Ki cost at all → 5. A grab that steals Ki when it lands: 5 bars → 4, 2 bars → 3. A skill that charges Ki, by how the source ranks its speed: the slowest → 3, the middle one → 4, the fastest of the three standard charges → 5. Ki Auto-Recovery that is always on → 4; a large one that needs a trigger → 3. A QQ Bang's top Max Ki boost (5, two more bars at levels 50-99, wiki) → 3: a bigger pool, not faster recovery. |
| Control | Guaranteed guard and stamina break → 5; a guaranteed guard break on its own → 4; a grab → 4; a counter, an unblockable hit, or holding the target in place (a freeze, sleep or forced dance) → 3; a slow-down, knockback only, or heavy stamina damage to a blocking opponent → 2; nothing recorded beyond damage → 1. |
| Mobility | A buff to movement speed → 4; a temporary buff to one kind of movement only, or of a size the source doesn't state → 3; teleports behind the opponent → 3; teleports only as a follow-up, or a built-in jump, dash or move (back, to the side, or to a safe distance) → 2. A speed buff of only a few percent is not graded as a buff. |
| Crowd handling | Only when a source says it covers an area: an explosion that hits every enemy nearby → 3; "a wide range" with no area damage recorded → 2. |
| Survivability | A QQ Bang's top health boost (5, about +1,500 HP, roughly 15% of a bar) → 3; an escape that knocks away or stuns the attackers around you, or a barrier that blocks every attack while it is up → 3; blocks some incoming attacks, an escape that only moves you out of reach, a defensive Limit Burst, or temporary Super Armor (no flinching, the damage still taken) → 2; damage taken from stamina instead of health for a time → 3; makes you take more damage, or leaves you tired afterwards → 1. |
| Stamina safety | A QQ Bang's top stamina boost (5, about +2 bars) → 4; +5% stamina recovery → 2; a skill that restores one stamina bar whenever you use it → 3; a Power Up's temporary +20% or more to stamina recovery → 3; damage that drains your stamina while your regeneration is reduced → 1. A grab that steals stamina when it lands: 2 bars → 3, 1 bar → 2. An Evasive's cost (100 stamina is one bar): 1 bar → 4, 2 bars → 3, 3 bars → 2, 4 or more → 1. |
